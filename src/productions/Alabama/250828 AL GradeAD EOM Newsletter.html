August Newsletter
 

Alabama lawmakers are trying to strip free speech and free expression from Alabama students and teachers, but we want you to know: YOU STILL HAVE RIGHTS! As we continue to battle Alabama's discriminatory censorship law, SB129, in the courts, we've published some Know Your Rights guides on our social media channels to get students, teachers, and faculty the information they need in the classroom now. Check them out and share them with your community:

  • Know Your Rights on navigating immigration policy in public schools – Instagram | Facebook
  • Know Your Rights as an LGBTQ+ student or teacher – Instagram | Facebook
  • Know Your Rights on public university campuses – Instagram | Facebook

There is a continued fight for justice in our state. We won't stop fighting until the rights of all Alabamians are honored and protected. From Legal, to Development, to Policy & Organizing, and Communications – your rights are our priority.

In The News

  • Voting Rights: A federal court has found that Alabama's state senate map violates the Voting Rights Act in Montgomery, but upheld the current map makeup in the Huntsville area. The decision is an important victory for Black voters, though Alabama still has work to do to ensure its maps provide fair representation. Read our full statement on our website.
  • Criminal Legal Reform: This month, we hosted the first session of our FREE workshop series, Atoning Alabama, educating Alabamians on the parole and pardon hearing process. Check out the coverage of this impactful event on WSFA.
  • Free Speech: A U.S. District Court declined to issue a preliminary injunction to block the enforcement of Alabama's discriminatory censorship law, SB129, in the state's public colleges and universities. This decision is a setback for professors and students at Alabama's public universities, but this fight is not over. Read our full statement on our website.
  • Celebrating 60 Years in the Fight: The FY 2025 Annual Report is here! Want to learn more about the amazing work that our team has done in the courts, in the legislature, and in the community? Read the full report on our website.

Upcoming Events

  • Moms Matter Listening Sessions in Shelby Co. on Saturday, September 13: Our maternal health listening sessions are in full swing! These listening sessions are for all Alabamians with experience at any stage of the reproductive journey – from family planning, to finding adequate pregnancy care, to dealing with pregnancy loss. Participants will receive a $75 gift card on a first-come, first-served basis. Can't make it to Shelby County? Read more about this campaign and sign up to attend a future session our website.
  • Atoning Alabama Workshop in Tuscaloosa on Saturday, September 27: We are proud to announce our newest campaign, Atoning Alabama – a FREE workshop series educating Alabamians on the pardons and parole process. Learn how to support incarcerated loved ones during hearings and navigate the complex parole and pardon systems at one of these workshops.
  • Project move, our voter outreach program aimed at building community, engaging new voters, and increasing voter turnout, is back in action! Stay tuned to this space as we announce Project MOVE stops in your community and ways you can get involved.
