{include "first_name"} – Like so many of you, the ACLU of Alabama team attended the No Kings Rallies that garnered thousands of attendees across the state, from Fairhope to Dadeville to Montgomery.

It was inspiring to stand alongside thousands of Alabamians exercising their First Amendment rights, and with over two million participants across 2700 rallies globally, it's clear that the people are ready for change.

But it is equally clear that without intentional strategy and direction, the change we need will be impossible. We must treat these No Kings Rallies as an entry point while understanding that this moment requires a deeper investment in the work being done on the ground.

As we build off the energy of these rallies, it's important we focus on actionable next steps, while following the lead of those who are already doing this work in community. Keep reading for actionable ways to channel your energy and lock in for the work ahead.

In The News

  • On the Blog: Following her remarks at the No Kings Rally in Birmingham on October 18, our executive director, JaTaune Bosby Gilchrist, penned a directive and a roadmap for Alabamians looking to take action beyond the rallies.
  • Notes from the Field: The Black Queer Visionaries Summit was a huge success! Read about the joy, connection, and unapologetic authenticity that radiated through the entire weekend from our LGBTQ+ Rights organizer, Kamilah Kenyatta.

Upcoming Events

  • Atoning Alabama Workshop in Choctaw Co. on Monday, November 3: We are proud to offer this FREE workshop series, Atoning Alabama, educating Alabamians on the pardons and parole process. Learn how to support incarcerated loved ones during hearings and navigate the complex parole and pardon systems at one of these workshops.
  • Moms Matter Listening Sessions in Anniston on Saturday, November 8: Our maternal health listening sessions are almost over! These listening sessions are for all Alabamians with experience at any stage of the reproductive journey – from family planning, to finding adequate pregnancy care, to dealing with pregnancy loss. Participants will receive a $75 gift card on a first-come, first-served basis. Can't make it to our last one? Read more about this campaign and how you can share your story on our website.
  • Happy Hour with the ACLU of Alabama in Birmingham on Thursday, November 13: Join us at Trim Tab Brewing in Birmingham for a happy hour benefiting the ACLU of Alabama! Come hang with local ACLU supporters and hear about the transformative work our staff is doing in community and in the legislature.
  • Save the date for Werk The Lege on Saturday, January 24, 2026: Mark your calendars for our annual Werk The Lege community teach-in! Registration details coming soon.
