{date("MMMM")} Newsletter
 

{include "first_name"}, this month has been heavy for our nation. And on the state level, we're witnessing tactics that aim to chill Alabamians' free speech and suppress our vote. As we weather these attacks together, we want you to know that the ACLU of Alabama won't stop fighting until the rights of all Alabamians are honored and protected. As freedoms' watchdog for over 60 years, we remain committed to defending the civil liberties guaranteed to us by the Constitution and the Bill of Rights. Here's what we've been up to this month in service of making sure "We the people" means ALL Alabamians.

In The News

  • Free Speech: In the wake of the killing of Charlie Kirk, an organized political effort has emerged to mine social media for statements about the killing and demand that state employees be fired or face other disciplinary action because of their speech. Read how we're responding on our website.
  • Voting Rights: Our Voter Protection Hotline received multiple reports of restricted access to fair, free, and safe elections during the August 5 and the August 26 Municipal Elections. We issued a report addressing these widespread failures that jeopardize Alabamians' fundamental right to vote. You can read it on our website.

Upcoming Events

  • Moms Matter Listening Sessions: Our maternal health listening sessions are in full swing! These listening sessions are for all Alabamians with experience at any stage of the reproductive journey – from family planning, to finding adequate pregnancy care, to dealing with pregnancy loss. Participants will receive a $75 gift card on a first-come, first-served basis. Can't make it to Cleburne Co.? Read more about this campaign and sign up to attend a future session on our website.
  • Atoning Alabama Workshop in Huntsville on Friday, October 10: We are proud to offer this FREE workshop series, Atoning Alabama, educating Alabamians on the pardons and parole process. Learn how to support incarcerated loved ones during hearings and navigate the complex parole and pardon systems at one of these workshops.
  • Project MOVE is back on their HBCU Tour! Come tailgate with the Project MOVE team at the Alabama State Homecoming game or at the Tuskegee Morehouse Classic on Saturday, October 4, and learn how we are moving voters to the polls in 2026 and beyond!

We invite you to continue to follow these monthly newsletters so you can stay in the know about what's going on in Alabama. Forward this email to a friend you think should be in the loop. And make sure to follow us on Instagram, X, and Facebook to get updates of what's happening at the Alabama State House on your timeline!
