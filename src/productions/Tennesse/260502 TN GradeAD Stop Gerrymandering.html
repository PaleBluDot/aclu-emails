{include "default_salutation"} –

At President Trump's urging, Governor Lee has announced that he is calling a special legislative session beginning on Tuesday, May 5, to redraw congressional district maps, with the specific goal of targeting the state's only majority-minority district in Memphis.

The special session comes on the heels of a devastating Supreme Court ruling on Wednesday that eviscerated Section 2 of the Voting Rights Act, permitting states to use partisan gerrymandering as a wholesale excuse to deny Black voters a voice in their government.

Take action against partisan gerrymandering that suppresses the vote of Black Tennesseans. Tell your legislators you oppose redrawing congressional maps for partisan gain.

Take Action

Partisan gerrymandering, no matter which side does it, hurts voters and undermines democratic representation by elected officials. Voters should be able to choose their leaders – not the other way around.

Calling a special session and redistricting at this point in an election year presents many logistical challenges, and it is a blatant power grab that puts politicians' self-interest before voters and our communities.

This effort to break a majority Black district significantly impacts our democratic process and aligns Tennessee with a dangerous and discriminatory practice of voter suppression, with voters of color feeling the brunt.

If voters are unhappy with their representative, they should have the power to change it. If politicians can manipulate the election, the people lose power, and our democracy suffers.

Contact your legislator today and tell them you oppose redistricting in Tennessee.

Our democracy works when all Tennessee residents have a right to elect leaders who reflect their communities' priorities. Politicians should meet those demands rather than changing the rules to their own advantage.

Thank you for joining us to protect democracy,
