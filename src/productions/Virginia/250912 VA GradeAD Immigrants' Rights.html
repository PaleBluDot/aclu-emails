Imagine you're one of 80 people in a room. You have no bed. It's so full you have to sleep sitting up. There are only two toilets. You get one burrito a day. You can't shower, nor can you access your medication or lawyer.

These are the inhumane conditions people are currently experiencing at ICE's Washington Field Office in Chantilly, Virginia.

How did this happen?

ICE has had a facility in Chantilly, Virginia, for years, but it's an office building meant to temporarily hold people, not a detention center, and it's not designed to hold people for longer than 12 hours at a time.

But we're hearing reports that people are being held there for more than a week at a time. Why? The Trump administration is determined to rip families apart.

Our lawyers are investigating the situation, and they sent the Department of Homeland Security and ICE a letter with the following demands:

  • stop holding people for over 12 hours;
  • stop pressuring people to consent to deportation;
  • release everyone who needs medical care;
  • give people enough food, beds, showers, clean clothes, and space to lie down; and
  • let people confidentially access their lawyers.

The U.S. Constitution protects everyone – regardless of immigration status.

ICE must follow the law, not violate people's civil rights and civil liberties – or physical safety – to advance an inhumane political agenda.

We'll keep you posted.
