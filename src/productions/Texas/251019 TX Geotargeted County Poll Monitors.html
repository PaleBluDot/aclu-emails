What is a poll monitor?

Poll monitors are trained volunteers who observe and, when needed, report or help address problems at polling locations. Becoming a nonpartisan poll monitor is one of the most important ways to ensure our elections go smoothly and every eligible voter has the information they need to cast a ballot.

When voters need help, questions are often simple: "Am I at the right polling place?" or "Where should I go to vote?" More complex questions can be referred to our team of legal experts, who provide real-time support.

How can I become a poll monitor?

Sign up for one of our virtual trainings – hosted on Tuesdays and Saturdays in October and Saturday, November 1.

We need poll monitors to help ensure an accurate account of what happens during the election, especially if certain actors try to discredit the results.
