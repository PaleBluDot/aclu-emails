Election season is here, and the ACLU of Ohio is committed to ensuring voters are ready to cast their ballots. Every election is important, and voting down ballot is crucial, as local and state officials have a major impact on the day-to-day lives of Ohioans.

We're relaunching our FAQ: Ohio Elected Officials series, which includes information on key officeholders such as the Governor, Secretary of State, State Representative, State Senator, and federal positions in the U.S. Congress. In addition, we have new materials for local positions like county auditor, county commissioners, and more.

Our team completed a research deep dive to outline the roles and responsibilities of these state and federal leaders to spotlight their unique powers in the state.

Issues from immigrants' rights and free speech to reproductive care and voting access are on the ballot each year. Whether these stakeholders are voted for directly by the people or appointed by other government officials, Ohio voters play a critical role in electing these leaders to shape the future of our state.

See our full list of FAQs on Elected Officials and get ready to make your voice heard in Ohio.
