Litigation Updates

The ACLU Foundation of CT has been busy (well, busier than usual) this past month.

On September 16, our legal team filed a case regarding our First Amendment rights: Quinn v. Eucalitto. This case follows Connecticut residents and Visibility Brigade members Erin Quinn and Dr. Robert Marra, who have stood on local roads passing over I-95, holding pro-democracy signs that condemn the government overreach we have all witnessed. State law enforcement has threatened and detained several members of the Visibility Brigade, variously claiming the sign-holders are trespassing or illegally displaying distracting signs. The ACLU Foundation of CT is suing to force the defendants – Department of Transportation Commissioner Garrett Eucalitto and Department of Emergency Services and Public Protection Commissioner Ronnell Higgins – to abide by the First Amendment and stop threatening or prosecuting overpass demonstrators.

Follow our case page to stay updated on Quinn v. Eucalitto.

166 Legal Actions Against the Trump Administration

Across the country, the ACLU Nationwide and affiliates have filed 166 legal actions in response to the actions of the second Trump administration. These actions include lawsuits, Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) requests, amicus briefs, agency complaints, and more.

With your support, the ACLU is fighting for our most fundamental freedoms across the country. We continue to secure victories in issue areas such as…

  • Protecting our right to protest and free speech;
  • Safeguarding immigrants' rights and due process;
  • Challenging censorship in schools, libraries, and more;
  • Defending against attacks against transgender people; and
  • Resisting attempts of executive overreach and abuse of power.
