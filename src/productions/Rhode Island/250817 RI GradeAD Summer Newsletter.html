Summer Newsletter
 

"Civil liberties don't take the summer off" is more true than ever. We've been working hard on cases and community building, in RI and on a national level. Check out our digital summer newsletter, which is packed with information and updates.

Victories And Case Updates

We've been busy! We've filed six new lawsuits in the past three months, we won a case for a Woonsocket man who was illegally arrested and incarcerated, and we had victories in multiple federal lawsuits challenging the Trump Administration's grant funding restrictions that will hurt vulnerable people the most.

We keep tabs on local government and law enforcement actions, and when something seems amiss? Sometimes a well-worded letter can have tangible results.
