{include "first_name"}, the 2026 midterm elections are less than three months away – and the ACLU is laser-focused on ensuring every eligible voter can cast their ballot this fall.

That's why we're holding a series of training sessions from now through the election to help voters understand their rights, recognize attempts at voter intimidation, and access assistance at the polls.

RSVP to join us tomorrow, August 18, at 8 p.m. ET for a training on protecting our elections with our partners at Voto Latino and Hispanic Federation. Please note: This link will take you to a third-party website, mobilize.us.

When politicians try to rig election rules to cling to power, intimidate voters, or override the will of the people, they are attacking the cornerstone of our democracy. It's going to take all of us to defend it – and that's a mission we take seriously.

Voting is how we protect our freedom and hold our leaders accountable. Knowing your rights – and sharing that information with your neighbors, friends, and family – is the best way to ensure that our elections stay fair, free, and reflective of our communities.
