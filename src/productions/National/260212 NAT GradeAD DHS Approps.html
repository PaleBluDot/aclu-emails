ICE and Border Patrol continue to violate our rights and traumatize our neighbors – and they're asking for more money. Congress must act: Put an end to no-strings-attached funding for ICE.

{include "first_name"}, the accounts our clients and other Minnesota residents have shared about their experiences being harassed, abused, and attacked by ICE and Border Patrol are absolutely horrifying.

"The ICE agents dragged me out of the restaurant and put me into a headlock on the ground. I repeated, 'I'm a citizen, I have an ID.' The officer kept saying, 'That don't matter, that don't matter.'"

in a sworn declaration for Hussen v. Noem

We're in court against ICE – challenging their racial profiling, suspicionless stops, and warrantless arrests in Hussen v. Noem. We're putting these stories in front of the courts to end ICE's abuse against our neighbors. But Congress is up against a deadline this Friday where they'll have to decide whether or not to continue funding ICE and Border Patrol's abuses – and we need your help to rein in ICE.

Tell Congress: Stop ICE's attacks on our communities. No more no-strings-attached funding for ICE.

"They were trying to put me into the car. They had not identified themselves, or looked at my ID, which is what I would expect them to do. I did not know if they were legitimate law enforcement. I was worried that I was being kidnapped."

– S.O., a Minneapolis resident
in a sworn declaration for Hussen v. Noem

Our messages to Congress have already made a difference in the fight to protect our rights from ICE and Border Patrol. After ACLU supporters like you sent over 360,000 messages, we successfully flipped enough votes in the Senate to block a bill that would have funneled more money into these agencies without any reform.

Now, the Senate has until Friday to put forward and vote on a new funding bill for DHS – so we're demanding serious changes to protect people from the kinds of trauma already inflicted on our communities.
