{include "first_name"}, yesterday, ICE agents fired several rounds into Oscar Belgal's car. He was shot in the neck while his 5-year-old child sat in the backseat.

The Department of Homeland Security's only response has been to point fingers at so-called "sanctuary" cities and New York City officials. No independent investigation has been announced. They haven't drawn back ICE agents from our streets. They're still committed to a dangerous mandate that ICE arrest 19,000 people a week.

Just weeks after Wilber Rafael Garcés Pérez was shot in Texas, we're in the same place again.

Without immediate intervention, we fear this shooting won't be the last. Call on Congress to stop the ICE killings.

Take Action

This sadly comes as no surprise. ICE's violence isn't a bug – it's a feature. Masked agents in our streets regularly tear parents away from children, tackle people to the ground, and threaten people in the streets with tear gas and guns. In ICE detention, record numbers of people are dying from medical neglect and abuse – with reported numbers more than doubling under the Trump administration.

The violence is intentional. ICE won't stop unless we stop them.

Congress can't let this become our new normal. We need a completely new immigration system – one that isn't based on roving patrols racially profiling and attacking people in the street. We need a system that treats immigrants like people. President Trump and Stephen Miller's deportation force must stop.

Tell Congress: Stop the ICE raids. Stop the attacks on our neighbors. No more deaths at the hands of ICE.
